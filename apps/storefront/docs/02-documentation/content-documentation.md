## Tone of Voice `[draft — confirm]`
- **Confident, not boastful** — "engineered for Pakistan's most demanding workspaces".
- **Specific, not vague** — cite dimensions, materials, warranty terms.
- **Local + global** — mix Lahore roots with international design standards.
- **Never** use: cheap, discount-blast, ALL CAPS marketing, purple/AI-generic phrases.

## Content Types
1. **Product copy** — 60–80 word description, spec table, warranty note.
2. **Series story** — 150–200 word narrative + 4–6 H2 sections.
3. **Case study** — challenge / solution / result + gallery.
4. **Blog post** — 800–1,500 words, one H1, H2/H3 hierarchy, hero image.
5. **Service page** — hero promise, inclusions list, 5-step process, 6 FAQs.
