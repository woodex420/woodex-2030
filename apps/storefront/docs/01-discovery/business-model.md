# Business Model `[draft — confirm]`

## Overview
WOODEX is a Lahore-based premium furniture manufacturer serving both corporate (B2B) and retail (B2C) markets. It combines an owned manufacturing facility with a direct-to-customer digital storefront and WhatsApp-driven sales.

## Revenue Streams
| Stream | Share `[draft]` | Description |
|---|---|---|
| B2B Office Fitouts | 60% | Turnkey office furniture packages: workstations, executive suites, boardrooms, receptions. Sold via e-quotation and enterprise sales. |
| B2C Retail (Home Furniture) | 25% | Direct e-commerce sales — bedroom, living, dining, home office. Slide-out cart checkout with COD / bank transfer. |
| Custom Manufacturing | 10% | Bespoke design + build for interior designers, architects, hospitality projects. |
| Services (Design + Installation) | 5% | Space planning, 3D rendering, delivery, installation, aftercare — billed per-project. |

## Pricing Model
- Currency: **PKR** exclusively (no multi-currency).
- B2B: itemised quotes via the E-Quotation basket, **75% advance / 25% on delivery**.
- B2C: fixed retail prices with occasional series-level promotions.
- Custom: cost-plus with a design fee, quoted after consultation.

## Channels
1. **Website** — primary top-of-funnel + catalogue + quote builder.
2. **WhatsApp** — primary conversion channel; every PDP, quote, and CTA drops into WhatsApp with a prefilled message.
3. **Physical Showroom** — Lahore flagship (see `showrooms.tsx`).
4. **Email** — quote delivery, order confirmations.
5. **Referrals** — architects, interior designers, corporate procurement leads.

## Fulfilment Model
- In-house manufacturing → QA → dispatch from Lahore.
- B2C delivery nationwide (Pakistan) via 3PL partners.
- B2B: white-glove delivery + on-site installation by WOODEX crew.
- Standard lead times: 2–3 weeks retail, 4–8 weeks custom/B2B fitout.

## Warranty & Aftercare
- 5-year structural warranty on frames `[draft — confirm]`
- 2-year warranty on mechanisms
- Aftercare service (see `Warranty.tsx`, `services/after-sales-support`)

## Margin Notes `[draft — confirm]`
- Retail margin target: 45–55%.
- B2B project margin: 30–40% (volume discount tiers).
- Custom: 25–35% after design labour.
