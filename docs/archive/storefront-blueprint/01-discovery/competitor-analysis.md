## WOODEX Differentiators
1. **Only local brand with a true B2B e-quotation flow** (75% advance model, PDF/WhatsApp/Email delivery).
2. **HON-grade corporate aesthetic** applied to Pakistani manufacturing.
3. **WhatsApp-first sales** on every CTA — matches how Pakistani buyers actually decide.
4. **Turnkey services**: space planning, 3D rendering, install, aftercare — under one roof.
5. **Series storytelling** (Ek, Infinity, etc.) creates collectible product identity local rivals lack.

## Threats
- Interwood's national dealer network.
- Habitt's B2C ad spend.
- Imported Chinese office furniture undercutting on price.

## Opportunities
- No local competitor owns "premium office fitout in Pakistan" SEO.
- Content vacuum in workplace design thought-leadership.
- Export potential to GCC — Pakistani craftsmanship at competitive USD pricing.
