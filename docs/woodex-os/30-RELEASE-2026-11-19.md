## What changed for a caller: every shape

The addresses did not change. The answers did. The number of the request is `meta.request_id`; a create that is
answered from its first sending says so in the header `Idempotent-Replayed`.

| Address | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/leads` | `{ok, reference, message, handoff, whatsapp_url}` | `data: {reference, handoff}`, where `handoff` is `{token, expires_at}` or `null`. `expires_at` is UTC, ending in `Z`; it was Pakistan time. Takes an `Idempotency-Key` |
| `POST /api/v1/leads/whatsapp-handoff` | `{logged, delivery, whatsapp_url}` | `data: {delivery, whatsapp_url}` |
| `POST /api/v1/quote-requests` | `{ok, reference, status, message}`; the key of the request as `request_key` in the body; a second sending answered 200 | `data: {reference, status}`; the key as the header `Idempotency-Key`; a second sending answered 201, as the first |
| `GET /api/v1/catalogue/categories` | `{schema, categories, series}` | `data: {categories, series}`. A published category page hands out 22 named keys; six keys of the editor that the shop does not read are no longer handed out (`text_format`, `intro_text`, `bottom_text`, `carousel_sort`, `carousel_limit`, `product_ids`) |
| `GET /api/v1/media` | `{schema, items}` | `data: [{key, url}]`, with `meta.total` |
| The two addresses that hand out a picture | the picture | the picture; asked with a parameter they do not take, they refuse (400) before any record is looked up |
| Every refusal | `{message, errors}`, in the framework's words | `error: {code, message, details}` and `meta: {request_id}`. A failed robot check is `VALIDATION_FAILED` on the field `turnstile_token` |
| Anything else under `/api/v1` | a page of the website | 404 `NOT_FOUND`, or 405 `METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED` with the header `Allow` |
| The shop's own two bridges, switched off | 503 `{disabled: true}` | 503, `error.code` `SWITCHED_OFF` |

`POST /api/public/leads`, the older enquiry address, keeps its address and its shape; so do the old website's two
addresses. The contract test holds that too.

## 8. Performance observations

Measured in the sandbox, on MariaDB 10.2.11 on one machine; a host will give other figures.

- **Enquiries are written one at a time now.** Every enquiry waits for one row before it asks whether the person
  is known. One enquiry alone took 57 milliseconds; of two sent at once, the slower took 82 at most. Two baskets:
  60 alone, 76 at most.
- A create with a key writes one more row and reads it first. One enquiry sent twice at once: 52 milliseconds
  alone, 99 at most for the one that waits and is given the first answer.
- Keys are kept without an end date: one small row for every enquiry and basket. Clearing old ones is open
  (T-134).
- The two-writers job in CI takes four to eight minutes on each server.
- Nothing was measured on the reading addresses; their limits are as they were (120 a minute).
